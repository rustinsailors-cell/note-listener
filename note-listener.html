Range check

About two minutes. Warm up first. For each step, hold the note for one or two seconds; slides and cracks are ignored. Use live listening or record a clip for each step. Skip any step you like.

    Key helper

    Find the karaoke key that fits your voice. Tell it the song's original key and its lowest and highest notes, then sing the song where it feels comfortable. It tells you how many half steps to shift and which key to buy.

    The original song

    Write notes like G3, Bb2 or F#4. Middle C is C4. Vocal range sites often list a song's lowest and highest sung notes, or play the song and read them with Read a recording above.

    How you sang it

    Sing the song, or just its lowest and highest lines, in the key that feels good. Held notes count. You can fix the notes by hand.

    Take it with you

    Saves a copy of this tool as one file. Open it in Chrome on your Mac to listen live, or follow the Mac setup notes Claude gave you.
